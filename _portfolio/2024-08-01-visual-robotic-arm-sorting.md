This project was completed for the China College IC Competition from Mar 2024 to Aug 2024 and received a national-level prize.

The system uses a Phytium-pi development board with a robotic arm, depth camera, conveyor-belt setup, and peripheral sensors to identify, grasp, and sort objects based on their categories.

My responsibilities included implementing and optimizing object-category recognition and 3D depth detection functions for the visual camera, along with sensor selection and layout.
